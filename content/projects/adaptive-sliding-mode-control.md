## Research problem

Industrial processes with dominant lag and dead time are challenging to control using fixed parameter settings.

## Approach

The work introduces an adaptive sliding-mode tracking controller with online gradient-descent updates of its parameters. The study compares tracking performance against fixed and alternative tuning approaches.
