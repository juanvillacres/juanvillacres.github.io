## Research problem

During continuous nut harvesting, material from successive trees can overlap on the conveyor. This makes assigning the measured yield to a single tree difficult.

## Approach

The two-part study reconstructs tree-level yield from measured volume flow using deconvolution, then estimates the uncertainty and links yields to individual trees.
