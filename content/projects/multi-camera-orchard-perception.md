## Research problem

Fruit occlusion in orchard canopies limits the visibility available to robotic harvesting systems.

## Approach

This research evaluates multiple RGB-D camera poses and their contribution to fruit detection in a V-trellised apple orchard.

## Publication

[Computers and Electronics in Agriculture (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compag.2024.109164)
