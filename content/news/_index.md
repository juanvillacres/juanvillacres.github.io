## 2026 NEWS 🚀
- **October:** We published our two-part paper in the Q1 journal Biosystems Engineering. Tree-level yield mapping for nut trees harvested with shake-catch harvesters. **Part I** introduces a volume flow deconvolution method to mathematically reconstruct continuous yield flow from delayed harvester sensors. **Part II** establishes a probabilistic framework to handle measurement uncertainties and accurately assign those yields to individual trees.
    [Paper 1 Link](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1537511026002114)

    [Paper 2 Link](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1537511026002254)
- **July-August:** We participated in the course "Training on Semiconductor Manufacturing Processes and Packaging Technologies" in Taiwan.
- **July:** We presented our paper, Adaptive Sliding Mode Control for FOPDT Systems via Online Gradient Descent Tuning, at CoDIT 2026. This work introduces an online-tuned sliding-mode controller that automates parameter tuning using gradient descent, significantly improving tracking performance for industrial processes with time delays without relying on fixed tuning rules. [Paper Link](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/11630768) [Conference Website](https://www.codit2026.com/index.php/accueil)
